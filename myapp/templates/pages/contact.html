Before you write in

How long until I hear back?

Most messages get a reply within one business day. Urgent, on-site issues during an active stay should go to the 24/7 line instead of the form.

Can I change my booking dates myself?

Yes — date changes on upcoming stays can be made from your account under "My bookings," subject to the property's change policy. Use the form only if the option isn't available online.

What should I include for a refund request?

Your booking reference, the dates involved, and a short description of what happened. This lets us look into it without a back-and-forth.

I want to list my property — where do I start?

Send a note to the partners address with your property's location, type, and rough availability, and the onboarding team will follow up with next steps.
